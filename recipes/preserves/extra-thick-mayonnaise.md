# Homemade Extra-Thick Mayonnaise

## Ingredients

- 3 egg yolks
- 1 heaped tsp Dijon mustard (1 level tsp = 5 ml)
- 300 g neutral oil, such as sunflower or grapeseed oil
- 1 tsp salt (5 ml), or to taste
- ¼ tsp ground pepper (1.25 ml), optional
- 30-40 g lemon juice, to taste

## Instructions

### Small food-processor method

1. Place the egg yolks and mustard in a small food processor whose blades reach the mixture, then blend for 30 seconds.
2. With the motor running, add the oil in a very slow, thin stream, taking particular care during the first 30 seconds to let each addition emulsify before adding more.
3. Once a quarter to half of the oil has been incorporated and the mayonnaise is thickening, add the remaining oil in a slightly faster, steady stream while continuing to blend.
4. Blend in the salt, optional pepper and 30 g lemon juice, then taste and add up to 10 g more lemon juice as needed.
5. Transfer to a clean, covered container and refrigerate for a couple of hours before using.

### Thermomix method

1. Fit the butterfly whisk and add the egg yolks and mustard to the mixing bowl.
2. Weigh the oil into a separate jug.
3. Mix the yolks and mustard for 30 seconds on speed 4.
4. Continue mixing on speed 4 while drizzling the oil through the lid opening in a very slow, thin stream, especially during the first 30 seconds.
5. Once a quarter to half of the oil has emulsified, add the remaining oil in a slightly faster, steady stream while continuing to mix.
6. Mix in the salt, optional pepper and 30 g lemon juice, then taste and add up to 10 g more lemon juice as needed.
7. Transfer to a clean, covered container and refrigerate for a couple of hours before using.
