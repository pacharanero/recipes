# Apple Chutney

## Ingredients

- 1kg peeled, chopped onions
- 1 litre malt vinegar
- 500g sultanas
- 2kg peeled, cored and chopped apples
- 1 tbsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1 tbsp salt; 1kg sugar
- finely diced carrot, courgette, and cauliflower

## Instructions

1. Place the onions in the pan with a little of the vinegar and cook
   gently until soft, watching carefully as it will tend to stick and burn if not circulated and stirred well.

2. Add the apples, sultanas, spices and half the
   remaining vinegar to the pan. Cook slowly, stirring and scraping the base of the pan regularly, until soft and dark brown. More liquid is sometimes required depending on the type of apples (cookers are drier)

3. Add the salt, sugar and remaining vinegar, stir until sugar is dissolved and continue cooking until thick. Add the finely diced vegetables to cook gently so they are cooked but remain crunchy.

4. Pot into warm sterilised jars and seal.

## 2026 Version 2: Post-Wedding Leftover Clear-Up Pickle

### Ingredients

- 1 orange, peeled and blitzed
- 5 apples, roughly chopped
- 5 Conference pears, blitzed
- 2 carrots, peeled and finely diced
- 2 litres apple juice
- 1 litre pineapple juice
- 500 ml orange juice
- 3 celery sticks, finely sliced
- 3 shallots, finely sliced
- 3 onions, finely sliced
- about 1 kg dark brown soft sugar
- about 500 ml red wine vinegar
- 1 tbsp tinga paste
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 2 tbsp whole allspice, freshly ground with a pestle and mortar
- 1 tbsp ground black pepper
- 4 tsp cornflour mixed with water to make a slurry

### Instructions

1. Put the onions, a little of the apple juice and all the other juices into a large pan. Bring to the boil.
2. Gradually add the orange, apples and pears, then cook until the liquid has reduced and the apples have broken down. Reserve the celery, carrots and shallots so they retain some texture.
3. Stir in the sugar and continue reducing the mixture.
4. Add the celery, carrots, shallots, tinga paste, cinnamon, allspice and black pepper. Continue cooking until the pickle is thick, stirring frequently and scraping the base of the pan to prevent sticking. A rolling boil helps circulate the vegetable pieces but still needs close supervision.
5. Test the consistency by chilling a small amount on a cold dish. When it is not watery, stir in the cornflour slurry and cook for a few more minutes.
6. Spoon into warm sterilised jars while hot and seal.

### Storage

Refrigerate after cooling and use within 4 weeks. The acidity and hot-fill process for this version have not been validated for shelf-stable storage.

### Serving

Serve with mature Cheddar or other strong vintage English cheeses, apples, grapes and biscuits.
