# Thick Sweet Chilli Sauce

A glossy, sticky sweet chilli sauce with a medium heat. It is thick enough to cling to spring rolls, chicken, tofu or noodles.

## Ingredients

### Small batch (about 400 ml)

- 120 g red jalapeño or other medium-hot red chillies, stems removed and deseeded
- 2 red bird's-eye chillies, stems removed, 1 deseeded
- 20 g fresh ginger, peeled and roughly chopped
- 4 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 125 ml rice vinegar or white wine vinegar
- 125 ml water
- 200 g caster sugar
- 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 2 tsp cornflour
- 2 tbsp cold water

### Large batch (about 2 litres)

- 600 g red jalapeño or other medium-hot red chillies, stems removed and deseeded
- 10 red bird's-eye chillies, stems removed, 5 deseeded
- 100 g fresh ginger, peeled and roughly chopped
- 20 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
- 625 ml rice vinegar or white wine vinegar
- 625 ml water
- 1 kg caster sugar
- 2 1/2 tsp fine salt
- 10 tsp cornflour
- 150 ml cold water

## Instructions

1. Choose either the small or large batch. Put the chillies, ginger, garlic, vinegar and water in a blender or food processor, then blend to a fine, slightly textured puree.
2. Transfer the puree to a suitably sized saucepan. Stir in the sugar and salt, bring to a simmer, then cook over a low-medium heat for 12-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until slightly reduced.
3. Mix the cornflour with the cold water to make a smooth slurry. Stir it into the sauce, bring back to a full boil, and cook for 1 minute until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon.
4. Pour into clean, sterilised jars and leave uncovered until completely cool. Seal and refrigerate.

## Storage

Keep refrigerated and use within 1 month. This recipe is intended for refrigerated storage, not ambient pantry storage.
