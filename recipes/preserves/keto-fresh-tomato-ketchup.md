# Keto Fresh Tomato Ketchup

Makes about 400 ml.

## Ingredients

- 1 kg ripe tomatoes, preferably plum or Roma
- 1 tbsp olive oil (15 ml)
- 1 small onion, finely chopped (about 60 g)
- 2 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tsp salt (5 ml)
- ½ tsp mustard powder (2.5 ml)
- ¼ tsp ground allspice (1.25 ml)
- ¼ tsp smoked paprika (1.25 ml), optional
- ¼ tsp ground black pepper (1.25 ml)
- Pinch of ground cloves
- 60 ml apple cider vinegar
- 30 g erythritol, preferably powdered
- Pinch of stevia powder, to taste

## Instructions

1. Core the tomatoes and roughly chop them, leaving the skins and seeds in place.
2. Warm the olive oil in a wide, heavy-based pan over a low heat and gently cook the onion and garlic for about 5 minutes, until soft and translucent without browning.
3. Add the tomatoes, salt, mustard powder, allspice, optional smoked paprika, pepper and cloves, then bring to a simmer, partly cover and cook for about 30 minutes, until the tomatoes have completely collapsed.
4. Blend until smooth with a stick blender, then push through a fine sieve with the back of a ladle to remove the skins and seeds.
5. Return the purée to the rinsed pan and stir in the vinegar and erythritol.
6. Simmer uncovered for about 45 minutes, stirring more often as it thickens, until it coats the back of a spoon and a line drawn through it on a plate holds; watery tomatoes may need longer.
7. Remove from the heat and add stevia a tiny amount at a time, stirring and tasting between additions, until sweetened to taste.
8. Adjust with more salt or a splash of vinegar if needed, then allow to cool for about 30 minutes and transfer to a clean, sterilised jar or food-safe squeezy bottle.

## Storage

Refrigerate and use within about 2 weeks, or freeze in an ice-cube tray for longer storage. This is a refrigerated condiment, not a shelf-stable preserve.

## Notes

- Erythritol provides body, while stevia adds sweetness without needing enough erythritol to make the ketchup gritty through recrystallisation in the fridge.
- Grind granulated erythritol to a powder in a spice grinder to help it dissolve.
- Stevia brands vary in strength and too much can taste bitter, so add it gradually.
- For a deeper flavour with bland tomatoes, roast them at 200°C for 30 minutes before adding them to the pan, or add 1 tbsp tomato purée (15 ml).
- For fewer carbohydrates, replace the fresh onion with ½ tsp onion powder (2.5 ml).
