# Sweet Chilli Sesame Dressing

A quick sweet, sharp and savoury dressing for green salads, noodle salads or slaws. Makes about 190 ml.

## Ingredients

- 60 ml sweet Asian chilli sauce
- 60 ml rice wine vinegar, or apple cider vinegar
- 60 ml toasted sesame oil
- 15 ml lime juice
- 2.5 ml ground ginger, or 7.5 ml fresh ginger, finely grated
- 5 ml Nam Pla fish sauce

## Instructions

1. Put all the ingredients in a clean jar with a tight-fitting lid.
2. Seal and shake vigorously until emulsified.
3. Taste and adjust with a little extra lime juice, fish sauce or sweet chilli sauce if needed. Shake again immediately before serving.

## Storage

Keep refrigerated and use within 2 weeks.
