# Quick Pizza Dough

A simple yeast-risen pizza dough that only needs a 5-minute rest before rolling - no overnight proving. Makes 2 thin-crust pizzas, ready in under 40 minutes. The dough can also be left to rise longer if you have time, but it's not required for a thin crust.

## Ingredients

- 350g tipo 00 flour, plus extra for dusting
- 1 sachet (7g) fast-action dried yeast
- ½ tsp caster sugar
- 200ml warm water
- 1 tsp salt

## Instructions

1. **Mix the dough.** Tip the flour into a large bowl and stir in the yeast and sugar. Make a well in the middle, pour in the warm water and add the salt. Mix with a wooden spoon until all the flour is incorporated and you have a soft dough.

2. **Knead.** Turn out onto a lightly floured surface and knead for 5 minutes until smooth and elastic.

3. **Rest.** Tip into a bowl, cover with a clean tea towel and set aside to rest for 5 minutes. (You can leave it longer if you have time, but it's not required for a thin crust.)

4. **Prepare the oven.** Heat the oven to 240C/220C fan/gas 8. Put a pizza stone, sturdy baking sheet, or upturned baking tray on the top shelf to heat up.

5. **Shape and bake.** If you've left the dough to rise, knock it back, then divide into two balls. Roll one out on a floured surface into a large circle, then lift onto a floured baking sheet. Add your toppings, then carefully slide the pizza straight onto the hot stone or tray in the oven. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the topping is bubbling and the base is crisp. Repeat with the second ball of dough.

## Notes

- Tipo 00 flour is finely milled and gives a softer, more pliable dough than strong bread flour. It's the traditional choice for Italian pizza.
- A hot baking surface is essential for a crisp base - preheat the stone or tray for at least 10 minutes.
- The dough keeps in the fridge for up to 24 hours if you want to make it ahead. Bring back to room temperature before shaping.
