# Keto Wraps

Makes 6 wraps.

## Ingredients

- 150 g (1½ cups) shredded mozzarella
- 30 g (2 tbsp) cream cheese
- 1 large egg, at room temperature
- 2 tsp (10 ml) baking powder
- ½ tsp (2.5 ml) xanthan gum
- 90 g (¾ cup) almond flour or almond meal
- Pinch of salt flakes

## Instructions

### Conventional method

1. Place the mozzarella and cream cheese in a large microwave-safe bowl and microwave on high for 2 minutes, until melted, or melt gently over a double boiler.
2. Working quickly, mix in the egg and baking powder until thick and foamy.
3. Fold in the xanthan gum, almond flour and salt, then work the dough in the bowl until evenly combined and no longer sticky.
4. Transfer the dough to a silicone mat and divide into three equal pieces.
5. Roll one piece between two sheets of baking paper to approximately 2 mm thick, then cut into rounds using a salad plate or 16 cm cake ring.
6. Add the trimmings to the remaining dough and continue rolling and cutting until you have 6 wraps.
7. Cook each wrap in a hot, dry frying pan for 20-30 seconds on each side, until lightly cooked.
8. Transfer the cooked wraps to a clean tea towel and wrap to keep warm and soft.

### Thermomix method

1. Place the mozzarella and cream cheese in the mixing bowl and chop for 5 seconds on speed 5.
2. Scrape down the sides of the bowl, then heat for 3 minutes at 90°C on speed 3.
3. Add the egg and baking powder and mix for 15 seconds on speed 3, then scrape down the sides of the bowl.
4. Add the xanthan gum, almond flour and salt and mix for 30 seconds on speed 3, then knead for 2 minutes on the dough setting.
5. Follow steps 4-8 of the conventional method to roll, cut and cook the wraps.
