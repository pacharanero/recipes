# Keto Fathead Dumplings

Makes 8 dumplings.

## Ingredients

- 150 g grated low-moisture mozzarella
- 100 g almond flour
- 1 large egg
- 1 tsp baking powder (5 ml), optional, for a lighter texture
- ¼ tsp salt (1.25 ml)
- 1 tsp dried mixed herbs or thyme (5 ml), optional

## Instructions

1. Put the mozzarella in a microwave-safe bowl and heat in 30-second bursts, stirring between each, until fully melted and smooth, about 60-90 seconds in total; alternatively, melt in a bowl over a pan of simmering water.
2. Stir the almond flour, optional baking powder, salt and optional herbs into the hot cheese until combined, then let it cool for a minute so the egg will not scramble.
3. Beat in the egg and knead until the dough is smooth and even; if very sticky, chill for 10 minutes.
4. With wet or lightly oiled hands, divide the dough into 8 walnut-sized balls.
5. Place on a lined baking tray and bake at 200°C (fan 180°C) for about 15 minutes, until golden and firm.
6. Sit the baked dumplings on top of a gently simmering stew, no more than half-submerged, then cover and cook for about 7 minutes to absorb some flavour without becoming soggy.

## Notes

- For a no-bake method, place the raw dumplings on top of a gently simmering stew, cover and cook for 15-20 minutes. Keep the simmer low and do not stir once the dumplings are in; they will be softer and more likely to spread or become gummy underneath.
- Use low-moisture block mozzarella, not fresh mozzarella in brine.
- For a softer, more tender crumb, add about 30 g cream cheese when melting the mozzarella.
