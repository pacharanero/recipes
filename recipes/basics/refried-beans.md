# Refried Beans

Simple homemade refried beans that work equally well as a party dip with nachos or as a side for a larger Tex-Mex meal. Serves 4 as a topping or 2 as a side.

## Ingredients

- 400g tin black, pinto or kidney beans
- 1 tbsp sunflower oil
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 green chilli, chopped (optional)
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- ½ tsp dried oregano
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- salt
- chopped coriander, to serve (optional)

## Instructions

1. **Prepare the beans.** Lightly drain the beans, reserving the liquid from the tin but not rinsing them - you want the beans in about half their liquid. Either mash roughly in a bowl for a textured finish, or blitz in a blender until smooth. Set aside.

2. **Cook the onion.** Heat the oil in a large frying pan or casserole and cook the onion for 8-10 minutes until soft and golden.

3. **Add aromatics.** Scatter in the garlic, chilli and cumin seeds and sizzle for 2 minutes more.

4. **Combine.** Tip in the beans, oregano, paprika and a pinch of salt. Cook for 5 minutes until heated through, adding more of the reserved bean liquid if it's too thick.

5. **Serve.** Serve straight away with a sprinkling of coriander, or leave to cool and keep chilled.

## Notes

- Will keep chilled for up to 3 days, or frozen for a month. Reheat with a splash of water mixed in.
