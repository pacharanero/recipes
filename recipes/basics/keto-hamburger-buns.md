# Keto Hamburger Buns

Makes 4 buns.

## Ingredients

- 140 g (1¼ cups) shredded mozzarella, not fresh mozzarella
- 56.7 g (2 oz) cream cheese
- 1 large egg
- 1 egg white
- 112 g (1 cup) blanched almond flour
- 2 tbsp coconut flour (30 ml)
- ½ tbsp baking powder (7.5 ml)
- ½ tbsp sesame seeds (7.5 ml)
- Water, for brushing

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to 190°C (375°F) and line a baking tray with baking paper or a silicone baking mat.
2. Place the mozzarella and cream cheese in a large microwave-safe bowl and microwave for 45 seconds, then stir.
3. Microwave for another 30-45 seconds, until melted and smooth, then allow to cool briefly if hot enough to cook the eggs.
4. Mix in the whole egg and egg white with a fork until well combined.
5. Stir in the almond flour, coconut flour and baking powder to form a dough.
6. Divide the dough into 4 equal portions, roll into balls and place on the lined tray, leaving space between them.
7. Gently flatten each ball, brush the tops with water and sprinkle with sesame seeds.
8. Bake for 12-15 minutes, until light golden brown.
9. Allow to cool for a few minutes before slicing in half.
