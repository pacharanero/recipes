# Crispy Air-Fryer Onion Rings

Crisp panko-coated onion rings with only a light coating of oil. Makes 4 side servings.

## Ingredients

- 2 large onions
- 40 g plain flour
- 1/2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 2 egg whites, lightly beaten
- 100 g panko breadcrumbs
- oil spray

## Instructions

1. Peel the onions and cut into 1 cm rings. Separate the rings and pat them thoroughly dry with kitchen paper.
2. Combine the flour, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper in a shallow bowl. Put the egg whites in a second shallow bowl and the panko in a third.
3. Dip each onion ring in the flour, then the egg white, allowing the excess to drip off. Press firmly into the panko until fully coated.
4. Preheat the air fryer to 200C for 3 minutes. Arrange the rings in a single layer, spray lightly with oil, and air fry for 8-10 minutes.
5. Turn the rings, spray the other side lightly with oil, then air fry for 6-8 minutes more until deeply golden and crisp. Cook further batches in the same way.

## Oven Method

1. Heat the oven to 220C/200C fan/gas 7. Put a wire rack over a baking tray and spray it lightly with oil.
2. Arrange the coated rings on the rack in a single layer, spray them lightly with oil, and bake for 10-12 minutes.
3. Turn, spray again, and bake for a further 10-12 minutes until crisp and golden.

## Notes

- Panko, a light oil spray and a single layer are essential for crispness. Do not crowd the basket or baking rack.
- Serve immediately, as the coating softens on standing.
