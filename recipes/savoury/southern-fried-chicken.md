# Southern Fried Chicken

Oven-baked southern fried chicken using skinless bone-in thighs and drumsticks. The coating is set and crisped in a brief pan-fry before finishing in the oven, which gives a genuinely crunchy crust without the skin. Serve with mustard slaw and paprika wedges.

Serves 8.

## Ingredients

- 8 skinless, bone-in chicken thighs
- 8 skinless chicken drumsticks
- 200ml milk
- dash of hot sauce (optional)
- 1 tsp salt, plus extra for the milk
- 200g self-raising flour
- 2 tsp onion granules
- 2 tsp garlic granules
- ½ tsp turmeric
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp ground white pepper
- 1 tsp cayenne pepper
- black pepper
- 6-8 tbsp groundnut oil

## Instructions

1. Heat oven to 200C/180C fan/gas 6. Put the chicken in a bowl, pour over the milk and add the hot sauce if using. Season the milk generously with salt.

2. Tip the flour, spices, 1 tsp salt and a good grinding of black pepper into a large plastic bag and shake to combine.

3. Heat half the oil in a large frying pan over a medium-high heat. Take half the chicken from the milk, shake off the excess, then drop into the bag of seasoned flour. Hold the top closed and shake well to coat. Lift out the pieces, shaking off any loose excess (but gently - don't strip the coating).

4. Lower the coated pieces into the hot oil and fry for a few minutes each side until golden and crisp. Don't overdo it - the chicken finishes in the oven. Use tongs to turn and try not to tear the coating. Transfer to a rack set over a roasting tin.

5. Repeat with the remaining oil and chicken.

6. Bake for 30 mins until cooked through. Check one thigh and one drumstick with a skewer - the juices should run clear. Cook for a further 5-10 mins if needed.

## Notes

- The two-stage cook (quick fry to set the crust, then oven to cook through) is key to getting a crispy coating on skinless meat.
- For thighs only, check doneness at 25 mins as they may cook faster than drumsticks.

## Sides

**Mustard slaw:** finely slice ½ white onion, grate 500g carrots, shred 1 small white cabbage. Mix with 2 tbsp mayo, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard and 2 tsp mustard seeds.

**Paprika wedges:** cut 1kg potatoes into wedges and boil in salted water for 5 mins. Drain, toss with 2 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp paprika and seasoning. Spread on baking sheets and bake alongside the chicken for 25-30 mins until crisp.
