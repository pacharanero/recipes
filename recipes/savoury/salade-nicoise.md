# Salade Niçoise

A classic Provençal salad with crunchy lettuce, ripe tomatoes, new potatoes, tuna, soft-boiled eggs and a punchy anchovy-mustard dressing. Serves 2.

## Ingredients

- 8 new potatoes
- 50g green beans, trimmed and halved
- 3 eggs
- 2 Little Gem lettuces, quartered
- 50g pitted black olives
- 2 medium tomatoes (plum are good), quartered
- 145g tin tuna in olive oil, drained, oil reserved

### Dressing

- ½ garlic clove
- 1 anchovy fillet (optional)
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 4 tbsp olive oil reserved from the tuna tin (topped up with extra if needed)
- salt and black pepper

## Instructions

1. **Make the dressing.** Mash the garlic and anchovy (if using) with a small pinch of salt on a board using the flat of a large knife, or in a pestle and mortar, until you have a paste. Stir in the mustard and vinegar, then slowly whisk in the tuna oil until combined. Set aside.

2. **Cook the potatoes and beans.** Tip the potatoes into a large pan of cold salted water. Bring to the boil, then reduce to a simmer. After 5 mins, add the beans and cook for a further 5 mins. Remove the beans with a slotted spoon and plunge into a bowl of iced water. Cook the potatoes for a further 5 mins until tender, then drain and leave to cool. Once cool enough to handle, halve or quarter them and toss in a large bowl with 1 tbsp of the dressing. Leave to cool completely.

3. **Cook the eggs.** Simmer the eggs in a separate pan for 7½ mins, then transfer to iced water. Once cool, peel and halve.

4. **Assemble.** Drain the beans and add to the bowl with the potatoes along with the lettuce quarters and olives. Add most of the remaining dressing and gently toss. Divide between two bowls and top with the tomatoes and eggs. Flake over the tuna, drizzle with the remaining dressing, and season to taste.

## Notes

- The oil from the tuna tin is integral to the dressing - use a good-quality tuna in olive oil.
- **Pan bagnat:** stuff any leftover salad (without the potatoes) into a split baguette with extra anchovies and a drizzle of olive oil. Weigh down with a tray for 30 mins, then cut into wedges and serve with the potatoes on the side.
