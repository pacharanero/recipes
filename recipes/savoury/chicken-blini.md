# Chicken and Mushroom Blini

_Also known as "Chicken blinchiki"_

## Ingredients

### Blini

- Approximately 375 g plain flour (3 cups)
- 840 ml buttermilk (3½ cups)
- 120 ml water (½ cup)
- 2 large eggs
- ½ tsp bicarbonate of soda
- 1 tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- Neutral oil, for greasing the pan

### Chicken filling

- 450 g cooked chicken, finely chopped
- 300 g mushrooms, chopped
- 2 medium onions, finely chopped
- 10 sprigs flat-leaf parsley, chopped
- 1 garlic clove, finely chopped
- 120 ml chicken stock (½ cup)
- 15 g butter (1 tbsp), plus extra for browning
- Salt and black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Whisk the eggs, flour, 240 ml of the buttermilk, bicarbonate of soda, sugar and salt together until smooth.
2. Whisk in the remaining buttermilk and water to make a batter with the consistency of double cream, then leave to stand for 20 minutes.
3. Heat a frying pan over a medium heat and lightly grease it with oil.
4. Pour in enough batter to coat the base thinly, tilting the pan to spread it to the edges.
5. Cook until the surface looks dry, then turn and cook for another 2–3 minutes until cooked through.
6. Transfer to a plate and repeat with the remaining batter, lightly greasing the pan as needed and stacking the cooked blini.
7. Melt the butter in a frying pan, add the onions and cook over a medium heat for 5 minutes.
8. Add the mushrooms, garlic and parsley and cook for about 8 minutes, until the mushrooms are cooked.
9. Stir in the cooked chicken and stock, season with salt and pepper, and cook for about 10 minutes, until the filling is piping hot and moist but not runny.
10. Spoon filling into the centre of each blin, fold the sides over and roll into an enclosed parcel, as for a burrito.
11. Fry the parcels seam-side down in a little extra butter, then turn and brown the other side, until the filling is piping hot throughout.
